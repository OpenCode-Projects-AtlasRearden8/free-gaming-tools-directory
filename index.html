Free companion tools for gamers

The games are free. Losing your saves shouldn't be the price of admission. This is a short, hand-curated list of companion tools — the stuff that lives next to your games: save managers, achievement and backlog trackers, controller mappers, screenshot organizers, and wiki note-takers.

Every entry includes what it checks, the platform, an offline-first flag, and a one-line verdict in plain English. No accounts, no ads, no favorites. See the curation policy.
